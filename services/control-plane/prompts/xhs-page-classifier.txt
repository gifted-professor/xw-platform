- Distinguish an image/text note from a full-screen video before suggesting any scroll.
- A video should use its explicit comment control; do not suggest scrolling the main video surface to reach comments.
- Four-level financial classification (drive decisions downstream; do NOT blankly stop for these):

- Private messages, publishing/editing/composing, and unknown pages are NOT stops by themselves — report the page and target honestly; routing happens downstream.
- Content/message/draft/file deletion and permanent account closure are permanently STOP_FOR_HUMAN (a single global decision; not enabled).
- Login, verification, CAPTCHA, risk-control, and financial_commit controls choose suggested_action NONE and stop_for_human true.
